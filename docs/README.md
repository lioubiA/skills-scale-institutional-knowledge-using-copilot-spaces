# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

A brief guide to the project management processes OctoAcme uses, with links to the detailed process documents.

## Project management processes

OctoAcme manages cross-functional work through an iterative lifecycle. Teams initiate work by confirming the customer or business need, success measures, stakeholders, and go/no-go decision. They plan approved work into a prioritized, estimated backlog with clear acceptance criteria, ownership, milestones, dependencies, and a Definition of Done. During execution, teams deliver in small increments using a project board, regular communication, reviews, testing, and risk tracking. Releases require passing checks, release notes, deployment verification, and rollback plans. Retrospectives capture lessons and track improvement actions.

Project Managers coordinate delivery, risks, schedules, and communications; Product Managers own outcomes and prioritization; delivery teams build and validate the work with stakeholder input. Teams use data to measure outcomes and continuously improve.
