# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.
Projects can combine or tailor these roles to fit their size and needs; the responsibilities should still have clear owners.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## UX / User Researchers

### Role Summary
UX / User Researchers investigate user needs and validate whether proposed experiences are usable and effective. They turn research findings into actionable insights for product decisions and delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Plan and conduct user research, and synthesize findings
- Identify usability needs and inform experience design
- Validate proposed solutions with users and share evidence-based recommendations
- Contribute usability goals and acceptance criteria

### Goals
- Ensure solutions address user needs
- Identify usability issues early enough to influence delivery
- Make user feedback accessible to the team

### Typical Communication
- Research plans, findings, and recommendations
- Usability reviews and design feedback
- Collaboration on discovery and acceptance criteria

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Partner with Product Managers to inform problem definition, prioritization, and validation.
- Work with Developers and QA/Testing to assess feasibility and define and verify usability criteria.
- Share findings with Project Managers to inform plans and surface research-related risks or dependencies.
- Engage stakeholders to understand user context and communicate findings.

## Data / Analytics Leads

### Role Summary
Data / Analytics Leads define how project outcomes will be measured and help the team collect and interpret reliable data.

### Responsibilities
- Translate success metrics into an instrumentation and measurement plan
- Define event, dashboard, and data-quality requirements
- Validate that reported metrics are accurate and useful
- Analyze results and communicate outcome trends and limitations

### Goals
- Make project outcomes measurable
- Provide trustworthy evidence for product and delivery decisions
- Enable teams to learn from results after release

### Typical Communication
- Measurement plans and instrumentation requirements
- Data-quality reviews and dashboards
- Outcome analysis and metric updates

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Work with Product Managers to define success metrics and interpret outcomes.
- Coordinate with Developers to implement and verify instrumentation.
- Collaborate with QA/Testing on validating data capture and reporting.
- Share measurement needs, dependencies, and findings with Project Managers and stakeholders.

---

## Security / Privacy Leads

### Role Summary
Security / Privacy Leads advise teams on security, privacy, and applicable compliance needs throughout planning, implementation, and release.

### Responsibilities
- Identify security and privacy requirements, threats, and risks
- Recommend proportionate mitigations and privacy safeguards
- Review relevant designs and controls, and help validate that requirements are met
- Escalate unresolved risks through the agreed project and security processes

### Goals
- Address security and privacy risks early
- Protect users and organizational data
- Make security and privacy readiness visible to decision-makers

### Typical Communication
- Risk assessments and mitigation recommendations
- Design and control reviews
- Readiness findings and escalations

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Advise Developers on secure implementation and QA/Testing on security and privacy validation.
- Coordinate with Product Managers to balance requirements and product needs.
- Work with Project Managers to track risks, mitigations, dependencies, and escalations.
- Inform stakeholders of material risks and readiness concerns through the appropriate channels.

---

## Operations / Support Representatives

### Role Summary
Operations / Support Representatives bring operational readiness and customer-support needs into project planning and delivery, and help teams prepare to run and support released changes.

### Responsibilities
- Identify monitoring, alerting, runbook, and support-readiness needs
- Assess operational impact and dependencies of proposed changes
- Contribute customer-facing support insights and known issue guidance
- Participate in release verification and feed operational learnings back to the team

### Goals
- Make changes supportable and observable in production
- Reduce avoidable customer impact during and after release
- Ensure support teams have the information needed to respond effectively

### Typical Communication
- Operational readiness reviews and runbooks
- Release coordination and post-deployment verification
- Support trends, incident learnings, and known issues

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Coordinate with Project Managers on release plans, readiness, and operational dependencies.
- Work with Developers and QA/Testing to define monitoring and verify operability.
- Partner with Product Managers on customer impact, support feedback, and follow-up priorities.
- Share support needs and release information with relevant stakeholders.
